## Symptoms
Queries on the Primary Database slowed sharply after roughly 2 million records were loaded; map refresh took over 30 seconds.

## Investigation
Execution plans showed sequential scans on the largest tables; index statistics confirmed no usable composite or spatial index for the new data volume.

## Root Cause
Slow queries after a large data load were traced to a missing index.

## Resolution
Added a composite index on the frequent filter columns and refreshed table statistics.

## Lessons Learned
Check index coverage before and after any large data load.
